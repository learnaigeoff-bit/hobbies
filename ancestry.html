Goodyear Tyre Factory Heritage

Bushbury, Wolverhampton, Staffordshire

The Goodyear Tyre & Rubber Company factory on Stafford Road in Bushbury was a central cornerstone of Wolverhampton's industrial landscape throughout the 20th century.

Both my mother and father were employed at the Bushbury plant for many years, participating not only in the industrial manufacturing process but also in the vibrant social club, annual sports galas, and community spirit that defined Goodyear families.

This archive section preserves photographs, retirement long-service badges, and oral history stories from the workforce era.

Samuel Fellows F.R.A.S.

Fellow of the Royal Astronomical Society

Great Uncle Samuel Fellows was elected as a Fellow of the Royal Astronomical Society (FRAS) in London during the early 20th century.

An avid observational astronomer, Samuel ground his own optical lenses and constructed brass reflecting telescopes. His observational logs contain accurate records of lunar craters, sunspot cycles, and planetary alignments across the West Midlands sky.

His dedication to public science included hosting telescope viewing evenings for local school groups and reading papers before astronomical assemblies.
